Your questions

Before we get started.

What does the free audit include?

An initial review of your publicly visible Google Business Profile, covering business information, services, photos, reviews and contact options. You receive practical recommendations and can decide whether you want help applying them.

Do I need to provide access to my Google account?

No account access is needed for the initial public-profile audit. If we agree work that requires profile access, we arrange an appropriate manager invitation. You keep ownership and do not need to share your password.

Can you help if I do not have a profile yet?

Yes. We can discuss profile setup and whether your business is eligible. Any verification requirements are handled through Google's process.

Do you guarantee a top position on Google Maps?

No. Rankings and enquiries cannot be guaranteed. The service focuses on accurate information, relevant service descriptions, useful content and clearer paths for customers to contact you.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on your profile, access requirements and the agreed scope. I confirm the expected schedule before work begins. Google's verification or review processes may affect when some changes appear.

Do I have to take monthly support?

No. You can choose the one-time optimisation only. Monthly support is optional, and its scope and terms are agreed before it starts.

Do you work with businesses outside Jersey?

Yes. I work remotely with local service businesses in Jersey and across the UK. We can communicate by email or WhatsApp.
