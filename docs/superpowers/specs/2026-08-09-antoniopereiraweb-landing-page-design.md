# Antonio Pereira Web — Landing Page Design

## Goal

Create a fast, English-only landing page for `antoniopereiraweb.co.uk` that converts UK and Jersey small-business owners into free Google Business Profile audit enquiries through WhatsApp.

## Audience

Local service businesses, especially cleaning companies, gardeners, trades and contractors, and beauty and wellness professionals.

## Positioning

Antonio Pereira is presented as a Local Visibility Consultant specialising in Google Maps, Google Business Profile, and local search. The page leads with the commercial outcome: more visibility, trust, calls, messages, and enquiries.

## Page Structure

1. Sticky header with brand, section navigation, and Free Audit CTA.
2. Hero with the headline “Get Found on Google Maps. Turn Local Searches Into Customers.”, supporting copy, primary WhatsApp audit CTA, and a Google Maps-style visibility visual.
3. Trust strip for UK and Jersey service businesses.
4. Problem section explaining why an incomplete or poorly optimised profile loses local enquiries.
5. Services and benefits covering profile setup/optimisation, local relevance, reviews/trust, and conversion improvements.
6. Target-sector cards for cleaning, gardening, trades/contractors, and beauty/wellness.
7. Three-step process: Free Audit, Optimisation, Growth.
8. Pricing with £500 one-time optimisation and £400/month growth service.
9. Honest results section using capability and deliverable proof only; no invented statistics or testimonials.
10. FAQ covering timelines, guarantees, access, service areas, reviews, and ongoing support.
11. Final conversion section with WhatsApp CTA.
12. Footer with `info@antoniopereiraweb.co.uk`, Privacy, and Terms.

## Conversion Rules

- Primary conversion is a WhatsApp conversation requesting a free visibility audit.
- No visible telephone number.
- CTA wording remains consistent throughout the page.
- Pricing is visible and easy to compare.
- Copy stays concise and outcome-focused.

## Visual Direction

- Clean, premium, Apple-inspired layout with generous spacing and strong typography.
- White and near-black base with restrained Google-inspired blue, red, yellow, and green accents.
- Rounded cards, subtle shadows, and light motion that does not slow the page.
- Mobile-first layout with a sticky/floating WhatsApp action.
- Original CSS visuals rather than copied Google interface assets.

## Technical Design

- Static HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript.
- No framework or build step.
- Semantic HTML, accessible controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- SEO title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, structured local service schema, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml.
- GitHub Pages deployment from the `main` branch.
- Custom domain configured through a `CNAME` file for `antoniopereiraweb.co.uk`.

## Reliability and Error Handling

- Navigation and CTAs remain usable without JavaScript.
- WhatsApp links open safely in a new tab and include a pre-filled audit request.
- If animation or JavaScript is unavailable, all content remains visible.
- External links use appropriate security attributes.

## Verification

- Validate HTML structure and internal links.
- Check mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts.
- Verify WhatsApp and email actions.
- Confirm no horizontal overflow and acceptable contrast.
- Confirm GitHub Pages deployment and both apex and `www` domain behaviour.
- Verify HTTPS after DNS propagation.

## Out of Scope

- Blog, CMS, login, payments, booking system, multilingual content, fabricated testimonials, and automatic Google Business Profile access.
